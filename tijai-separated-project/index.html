Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about requesting and tracking a delivery.

How do I request a pickup?

Choose a delivery service, describe the item, and add the pickup and delivery addresses and contact details. Submit the form to create a demo request.

How do I get a tracking number?

The preview creates a demo tracking number after you submit a request. In a live service, the number would be generated after the order is accepted and shown in your confirmation.

Can a rider see my request now?

This preview saves requests in the browser. It does not send them to a rider. A shared backend is needed for live dispatch.

Which towns can I choose?

The sample form includes Yola, Jimeta, Mubi, Numan, Gombi, and Hong. A live service should show only towns that are currently covered.

Terms for customers using TijAl Delivery

These terms describe the current proposed service. The website preview is for demonstration; submitting a request in the preview does not dispatch a rider or create a live delivery.

1. Who we are and where we operate

TijAl Delivery operates from No. 13 Bauchi Street, J/Yola, Adamawa State. We arrange pickups and deliveries across the Local Government Areas of Adamawa State, subject to route availability, safe operating conditions, and confirmation of each request.

2. Delivery requests

When requesting a delivery, give accurate descriptions of the item, pickup and drop-off addresses, and sender and recipient contact details. A request is accepted only when TijAl confirms it. The live service should provide an order confirmation and tracking number after acceptance.

3. Fees, estimates, and payment

Delivery fees and estimated times depend on the pickup and destination locations and the requested service. We will show the applicable price and estimate before you confirm. Payment methods offered at checkout may be used. Where payment is due on delivery, the item will be handed to the recipient only after all applicable charges have been paid.

4. Items and packaging

Describe items honestly and package them securely. We do not accept illegal items, explosives, weapons, flammable or hazardous materials, or other items prohibited by law. Prescription medicines may be carried only where lawful and any required prescription or documentation is provided. Cash, live animals, and unusually valuable or perishable items require prior written approval.

5. Changes and cancellations

Contact support as soon as possible to change or cancel a request. A request may be cancelled without a delivery charge before pickup, unless a separately disclosed and lawful cost has already been incurred. Once pickup has happened, costs already incurred may be charged if disclosed. If TijAl cannot complete an accepted delivery, we will contact you and arrange an appropriate refund or other remedy under the applicable policy and law.

6. Delays, loss, or damage

Delivery estimates are estimates and may change due to traffic, weather, access, safety, or other operating conditions. We will provide an update when we become aware of a material delay. Report missing or damaged items to support as soon as possible, with the order number and relevant details. TijAl will review the report and provide an appropriate remedy in line with applicable law. Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot legally be excluded.

7. Customer information and privacy

We use names, phone numbers, addresses, and order details to arrange deliveries, provide support, and help protect the service. We share only the information needed with the assigned rider and relevant service provider. We retain information only for as long as needed for these purposes or legal requirements, and take steps to protect it. See the Privacy Notice for full details and how to request access or correction. These practices should follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

8. Customer age and account use

Account holders must be at least 18 years old. A parent or guardian may arrange a delivery for a younger person and remains responsible for the request and the accuracy of its details. Keep account credentials private and notify support if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

9. Complaints and customer rights

Contact us by email or phone using the details below. Support is available 24 hours a day, and we aim to respond immediately; during busy periods, a response may take longer. We will review complaints fairly. These terms do not limit rights provided by applicable Nigerian consumer protection law. See the FCCPC consumer rights information.

10. Updates and contact

We may update these terms when the service or operating policies change. We will publish the current version and update the revision date. Contact TijAl Delivery at tijaldelivery@gmail.com or 08061211170, or visit No. 13 Bauchi Street, J/Yola.
